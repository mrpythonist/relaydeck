# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RelayDeck a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project
maintainers via a GitHub issue or the contact listed in SECURITY.md.
Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, and other contributions that violate this Code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
